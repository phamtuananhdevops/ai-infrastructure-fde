# Technical English for AI Infrastructure FDE

## Goal
Build practical spoken and written English for infrastructure incidents, customer deployments, technical explanations and interviews. **Not an English certificate course.** Listening is self-reported as adequate; **speaking is the primary weakness**. No formal assessment completed.

## Speaking-first routine (15 minutes, 5 days/week)
- 5 minutes: shadow a short relevant Udemy clip, repeating aloud.
- 5 minutes: explain a current technical concept without reading a script.
- 5 minutes: role-play with AI mentor as customer or senior engineer, with follow-up questions.
- One weekly speaking challenge tied to the active infrastructure topic; score clarity, structure, technical accuracy and response to follow-ups. Do not claim speech evaluation without actual spoken evidence.
- Listening practice is supportive, not the main focus.

## Coaching method
- Explain technical concepts in Vietnamese first; practice short English explanations of the same work.
- Start with simple, correct sentences, then upgrade to natural professional wording.
- Correct errors kindly but precisely: show original, corrected version and one short explanation.
- Focus on clarity and confidence, not accent imitation or advanced grammar.
- Use English subtitles and reduced playback speed for Udemy Business when helpful; don't count watching as practical completion.

## Weekly integration (8–10h total)
- Hands-on build/break/fix: 5h
- Targeted Udemy and documentation: 2h
- Technical English: approximately 1–1.25h, primarily speaking (15 minutes x 5 days)
- Runbook, reflection and GitHub evidence: 1–2h

## Progression
| Weeks | English task | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Explain request path, Linux checks and Docker setup in 3–5 simple sentences | Short written explanation |
| 5–12 | Describe Kubernetes incidents: symptom, investigation, cause and fix | Incident reports |
| 13–16 | Explain deployment pipelines, monitoring and rollback | Change update / runbook |
| 17–24 | Explain inference, latency, throughput and GPU sizing | Benchmark summary |
| 25–32 | Customer discovery, status updates, risks and handover | Role-play transcripts and deployment notes |
| 33–36 | Technical interviews and architecture presentations | Mock interview responses |

## Reusable communication patterns
- Symptom: "The service is returning HTTP 502 errors."
- Investigation: "I checked the application logs and the upstream connection."
- Hypothesis: "One possible cause is a backend connectivity issue; I will verify it."
- Customer update: "We are investigating the issue and will share the next update after validation."
- Recovery: "The service is healthy again, and we verified the health endpoint."

## Evaluation
Score practical communication on technical correctness, clarity, structure and improvement over time. Do not penalize an infrastructure lab for English grammar unless English communication is explicitly part of its rubric. No scores until evidence is submitted.
